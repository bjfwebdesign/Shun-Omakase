Chef's Philosophy

Chef Kenji Arata

Twelve years training under a sushi master in Toyosu before Chef Arata brought jukusei — the slow, deliberate aging of fish — to Den Haag. Nothing on the counter is served at its freshest. It's served at its best.

Fish is flown in from Toyosu three times a week, aged in-house for four to twenty-one days depending on the cut, and finished with select catch from the North Sea when the season calls for it.

— Chef Kenji Arata
